# סטודיו מוצר

מקור: Gem בשם "סטודיו מוצר" של גיא כהן. ההוראות למטה הועתקו מילה במילה.

Transform this product photo into a professional Amazon-style

e-commerce catalog image.

Requirements:

- Pure white background (#FFFFFF)

- Professional studio lighting: soft diffused light from both

sides with subtle natural shadow beneath the product

- Product centered, filling 85% of the frame

- 3/4 angle perspective showing depth and dimension

- Sharp focus on entire product, no blur

- Photorealistic commercial catalog quality

- Clean minimalistic look

- Neutral color palette with natural product colors preserved

- Macro product photography style

- Premium, polished finish — ready for online store listing

- 32k UHD quality

- Keep the exact same product, same colors, same details —

only upgrade the background, lighting, and composition
